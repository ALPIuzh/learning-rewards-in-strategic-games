Figure~\ref{fig:appendix-lobeke-diagnostics} uses positive-affine reward
representations in $[0,1]$, so that $R_{\max}=1$, without changing leader-policy
orderings. The left column compares active-design protocols that may choose the
leader policy used to elicit each poacher observation; the right column compares
fixed-expert estimators that instead assume observations from a source-task
expert. Corresponding rows use identical axes, while the separate legends keep
these data-access assumptions explicit. Below the labeled divider, transfer
metrics are averaged taskwise over a fixed deterministic suite of ten downstream
leader rewards and then across 50 evaluation seeds; bands are 95\% confidence
intervals conditional on that task suite. The pale region marks the first 50
observations used by warm-up variants.
