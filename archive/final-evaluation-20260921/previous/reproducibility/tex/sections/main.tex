\section{Numerical validation}

We compare reward-learning protocols in a GUARD-derived Lob\'ek\'e game with a
simulated logit-response follower. Active methods choose the patrol policies
used to collect observations; fixed-expert methods receive demonstrations from
a source-task equilibrium oracle.

Every evaluation leader reward is represented positive-affinely in $[0,1]$,
so that $R_{\max}=1$. This leaves all leader-policy orderings unchanged, and
the reported metric remains ordinary $\operatorname{SubOpt}$.

The retained experiments measure source-task performance and transfer to new
leader objectives without further follower observations. Warm-up and no-warm-up
Active IRL use different information multipliers, so their comparison alone
does not isolate the effect of warm-up.

These results concern one fixed game and a correctly specified response model.
The selected downstream objectives illustrate transfer within that setting;
they are not a sample of independent environments.
